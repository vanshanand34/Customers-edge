About the Project

Making price comparison simple.

Customer's Edge helps you compare products across online marketplaces so you can make better buying decisions without checking multiple websites yourself.

Customer's Edge

Shopping online often means opening multiple websites just to compare the same product. Customer's Edge brings those results together in one place.

Search for a product and get relevant listings, prices, ratings and product links from different marketplaces in a single view.
